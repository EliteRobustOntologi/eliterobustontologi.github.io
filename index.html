Questions, answered

Good to know before you start.

How long does a website take?

A small website may take around 5–7 working days. Larger sites can take 2–3 weeks or more. Timing depends on scope and how quickly required content and feedback are provided.

Do package prices include hosting and a domain?

Not by default. Domain, hosting, business email and paid third-party services should be confirmed in your quote. Some projects can use existing infrastructure or suitable free hosting options.

How do payments work?

A suggested arrangement is a 60% deposit before work starts and the remaining 40% before final handover or deployment. The agreed quote and terms will confirm the actual payment schedule.

Can you build an online store or custom system?

Yes. E-commerce, user accounts, payment integrations, dashboards and custom applications need a requirements review and a separate quote.

What do I need to provide?

Usually your logo, business information, page content, images, preferred contact details and any specific features. We can help define the structure if you're starting from scratch.

Will my website work on phones?

Responsive design is included in the website packages. We aim to make the layout and key actions usable on mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
